How the Template Importer Works

  • travel_explore
    Phase 1: Deep Site Crawl

    Audits all CSS, JS, fonts, media, meta tags, and discovers tracking scripts.

  • filter_alt
    Phase 2: Prune & Clean

    Uncheck unwanted analytics or third-party trackers with 1 click before building.

  • cloud_upload
    Phase 3: CDN Asset Migration

    Uploads kept stylesheets, fonts, and images to high-speed cloud CDN for persistent delivery.

  • branding_watermark
    Phase 4: Debrand & Blueprint Generation

    Replaces original brand names with your workspace identity and injects dynamic Ozikol CMS tokens.

  • verified
    Phase 5: Instant Adoption

    Installs the complete suite directly into your tenant database ready for live publishing.
