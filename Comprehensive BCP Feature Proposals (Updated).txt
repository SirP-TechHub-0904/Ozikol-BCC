# Comprehensive BCP Feature Proposals (Updated)

This document contains the functional business features for the Business Cloud Platform (BCP). It outlines the module taxonomy, core capabilities, console/workspace views, common routes, and build recommendations.

---

## Group 1: People, Community & Collaboration

These features support member communities, internal groups, alumni networks, messaging, and secure reporting.

### 1. Community Messenger & Team Chat

**Core**
Provides platform-wide instant messaging including 1-on-1 direct messaging, departmental/committee group channels, topic spaces, rich media sharing, @mentions, presence status, and real-time push notifications.

**Console**
Community and IT administrators manage chat spaces, set content moderation filters, manage channel permissions, archive channels, and review audit trails.

**Workspace**
Members and staff initiate private 1-to-1 chats, join public/private group channels, share documents and images, react with emojis, and access the floating quick-chat drawer from anywhere on the platform.

**Common**
Full-screen messenger view, floating quick-chat popover widget, channel roster partial, direct message thread, message attachment gallery, online status badge.

**Dashboard**
Required:
```text
/BcpChat/Console/Dashboard
/BcpChat/Workspace/Dashboard
```

**Common Routes**
```text
/BcpChat/Common/Thread/{id}
/BcpChat/Common/ChannelRoster
/BcpChat/Common/QuickChatDrawer
```

### 2. Committees & Sub-Groups

**Core**
Manages committees, boards, task forces, working groups, leadership rosters, meeting schedules, minutes, and resolutions.

**Console**
Community officers configure committees, assign chairs and members, schedule meetings, upload minutes, track resolutions, and monitor committee activity.

**Workspace**
Members view their assigned committees, access upcoming meeting details, download agenda/minutes, and participate in discussions.

**Common**
Committee cards, member roster partials, meeting calendar widgets, minute download links, resolution badges.

**Dashboard**
Required:
```text

/BcpCommittees/Workspace/Dashboard
```

**Common Routes**
```text

/BcpCommittees/Common/MeetingMinutes/{code}
```

### 3. Alumni Networking Directory

**Core**
Maintains an alumni and past-member registry with graduation years, chapters, career history, networking preferences, and verification status.

**Console**
Alumni administrators verify alumni profiles, manage chapters, post alumni bulletins, approve networking directories, and export reports.

**Workspace**
Alumni update their profiles, search for peers by industry, location, or graduation year, and connect with other members.

**Common**
